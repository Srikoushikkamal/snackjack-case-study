# 3. What I changed

Each change came from a problem in [Context and problems](01_context_and_problems.md). The evidence for each is in
[Findings](../reports/findings.md). The months are approximate.

| Problem | What I found | What I did | How I checked it |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Nobody knew us | Students need to taste something once to come back | Found a star dish for each spot: chicken kothu parotta at FoodJack, boba tea and 500 ml juice jars at SnackJack, and a range of dosas (podi, masala, potato, hot chutney, ghee podi masala, special spice) that became popular with hostel students. Launched trending items like boba tea and waffles, with Canva posters and a student WhatsApp community | Bills per day before and after each launch; the new item's share of sales |
| P2 Spots going quiet | Students move in groups: one friend likes another place and the whole group goes | Became friends with the students, kept regulars close through the WhatsApp community | How often bills dropped, and for how long |
| P3 One item and gone | Students respond to being talked to in their own slang | Suggested an add-on at the counter in their language; brought the rest of the menu back through these add-ons | Items per bill and average bill |
| P4 Waste on some days | Exams, holidays, admissions, power cuts and seasons change demand, but prep did not | Planned prep, and seasonal items for summer and winter, from the college calendar | Waste % by kind of day |
| P5 Dishes binned | Weighing before serving and before binning showed veg dishes were binned far more than chicken | Rebuilt the FoodJack menu around chicken | Share of food eaten against share binned, veg against chicken |
| P6 Engineering College waste | Some dishes do not survive the trip from the hub kitchen | Redesigned the Engineering College menu around dishes that travel well | Waste % for dishes that travel well against those that do not |
