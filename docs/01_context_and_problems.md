# 1. Context and problems

## The business

Snackjack Foods ran food spots inside college campuses in Coimbatore. When I joined in February 2023 it was one shop
selling meals and snacks together. I split it into two brands and grew it to five spots:

| Spot | Brand | What it sells |
|---|---|---|
| Arts College | FoodJack | Meals: biryani, kothu parotta, fried rice, noodles, dosas. Also the hub kitchen |
| Engineering College | FoodJack | Meals, cooked at the hub kitchen and sent over |
| Arts College Library Block | SnackJack | Snacks, juices, milkshakes, hot drinks |
| Boys Hostel | SnackJack | Same |
| Girls Hostel | SnackJack | Same |

Two competitors were already on campus with a loyal base before we started.

## Why business inside a campus is different

- **Short windows.** A 30 minute break and a 45 minute lunch. Most of the day's trade happens in about an hour and a
  half, with around 50 students in front of the counter at once.
- **The calendar decides demand.** Exams, holidays, admissions, scheduled power cuts and the seasons all change who is
  on campus and what they want.
- **Lots of alternatives.** College meals, food from home in a tiffin box, and restaurants just outside the gate.
- **Customers decide in groups.** Students are 18 to 22 and eat where their friends eat.

## The problems

| # | Problem as it showed up | Question I needed answered |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Students did not know us; the competitors had their base | What will make each spot worth walking to? |
| P2 | Some days a spot would suddenly go quiet for a few days | Why do students switch where they eat? |
| P3 | Students bought one item and left | What makes them buy more? |
| P4 | Too much food was binned, worse on some days than others | When is food wasted, and why? |
| P5 | FoodJack binned a lot of particular dishes | Which dishes, and why are students not eating them? |
| P6 | The Engineering College spot binned far more than the Arts College | What happens to food on the way from the hub kitchen? |

## People involved

| Who | What they care about |
|---|---|
| Students | Taste, price, speed in a short break, being where their friends are |
| Owner | Profit, the brand's name on campus |
| Staff at each spot | A clear routine, getting through the rush |
| Vendors | Clear orders on WhatsApp, payment every weekend |
| College and hostel administration | Exam timetable, holidays, rules for food on campus |
